Privacy

How we handle what you send us.

Last updated 26 November 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you give us through the form on aezmo.com. It is short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Aezmo is a management and representation agency for technology and fintech creators on X. In this notice, “we” and “us” mean Aezmo.

What we collect

Only what you type into the form on this site.

  • If you book a call as a creator: your name, X handle, email address, audience size, what you are known for and, if you tell us, what you want from management and when suits you for a call.
  • If you book a call as a brand: your name, company, work email, who you need to reach, your timing, when suits you for a call and anything else you add.

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

To read your request and reply to arrange the call. If we go on to work together, we also use it to set that up. We do not sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

A few services run parts of this site for us.

  • Formspree receives the form, emails it to us and keeps a copy in our account.
  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it handles technical details such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

These companies may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months, and then we delete it, unless we go on to work together.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Reply to any email we have sent you, or send us a note through the form on this site, or email . We will answer within a month.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
