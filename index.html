(01)Position

Our position

The most valuable voices in technology and finance are not media companies. They are individuals — builders, founders and analysts — with audiences these industries cannot reach any other way. X is where they think out loud. We represent the people they listen to: properly, selectively, and for the long term.

  • Selective

    A deliberately small roster. Everyone we represent works directly with the person running their account.

  • Full-service

    Brand, content, audience and commercial work, run as one programme with one point of contact.

  • Long-term

    We build careers and companies around a voice. Not one-off campaigns.

(05)Standard

The standard we work to

A public voice runs on trust, and trust is the one asset you can't buy back. So we work to a written standard — and we turn down whatever falls short of it.

We will

  • Disclose every paid partnership — clearly, every time.
  • Take on work only where it fits your audience and your name.
  • Put every term in writing, in plain language.
  • Report real numbers, including the ones that disappoint.
  • Clear every financial promotion with the regulated firm before it runs.

We won't

  • Run undisclosed promotion.
  • Touch token launches, trading schemes, or anything that needs your audience to lose.
  • Put your name to a product you haven't used.
  • Publish in your voice without your approval.
  • Buy followers, engagement or attention.

Questions, answered

Technology and fintech creators, and founders and operators, whose main audience is on X. We look at who is listening before we look at how many.

Four disciplines: personal brand, content, audience growth and commercial partnerships. Some clients need all four from the first day. Others start with representation and grow into the rest.

For commercial partnerships, yes. Brands need one point of contact, and we can only protect your rates if we see every offer. Terms are written in plain language and include a clear way out.
